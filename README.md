🚀 Project Overview

The analysis uses customer demographic and financial data to uncover:

Which customers are more likely to take loans

Which groups represent higher credit risk

How financial behavior changes over time

How age, tenure, and segment influence loan performance

The project includes a fully interactive Power BI dashboard, a presentation, and supporting documentation.

📊 Key Insights
🔸 1. Loan Ownership

Only 30% of customers currently hold loans.

Average loan size ≈ 94,000 EGP.
➡️ Large opportunity to grow lending among stable customer groups.

🔸 2. Loan Demand

40–50 age group makes up 31.7% of all loan holders.

Segment D customers hold 64.4% of all loans.
➡️ These groups should be targeted for new loan products.

🔸 3. Repayment Behavior

Late payments occur in 1.42% of customers.

63.6% of late payers are under age 40.
➡️ Younger customers are higher risk and need stricter screening.

🔸 4. Customer Stability

71% of customers are new (tenure < 1 year).

Long-tenure customers (>2 years) show 0.17% late-payment risk.
➡️ Tenure is a strong predictor of repayment reliability.
